# AGENTS.md — src/components/insights

- Insights (Vendas) view state lives in the URL query via `src/lib/insights/urlState.ts` (relative period presets, filters, foco/tv/zoom/atualizar) using history.replaceState; it never writes dashboard settings. Why: links open the same view on any device or TV without changing it for others.
- Seller company columns are opt-in through `rankingCompanyIds`; `useVisualData` partitions filtered records by the registered product's company (UUID, legacy key or unique exact option label), falling back to stored `deals.company_id` when unresolved. Why: ranking totals and product pills agree without rewriting historical sales, calls, other visuals or backend rules.
- Company rankings render one seller column per company with a shared 32rem maximum width for its header and seller list on all chart surfaces; `tvLayout` prioritizes seller rows over chart/Calls whitespace only in fitted TV boards, with stage safety padding measured and an independent v5 device preference. Why: cards must not stretch to the edges on wide screens; rankings fit without changing saved layouts, data or explicit URL overrides.
- Vertical stacked charts keep the legend in normal flex flow above a bounded plot and cap column widths; labels never intercept bar clicks. Why: actual legend height prevents overlaps at any zoom without changing saved layouts or aggregations.
- All ranking layouts resolve accents through `insightPaletteColors`, including compact company columns; product pills keep their separate presentation. Why: per-visual palette changes must reach progress bars and row accents without changing product colors or persisted data.
- Calls Commercial share left-aligned bounded seller sections with stretched identity rows and bottom-aligned metric blocks (number, label, conversion) across fitted TV, manual zoom, normal, preview and shared charts. Density uses untransformed client dimensions and overflow stays local. Why: names must align above their blocks without reordering the dashboard, stretching blocks or clipping content; counts, scopes and saved settings remain unchanged.
