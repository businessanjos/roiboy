# AGENTS.md — src/components/insights

- Insights (Vendas) view state lives in the URL query via `src/lib/insights/urlState.ts` (relative period presets, filters, foco/tv/zoom/atualizar) using history.replaceState; it never writes dashboard settings. Why: links open the same view on any device or TV without changing it for others.
- Seller company columns are opt-in through `rankingCompanyIds`; `useVisualData` partitions filtered records by the registered product's company (UUID, legacy key or unique exact option label), falling back to stored `deals.company_id` when unresolved. Why: ranking totals and product pills agree without rewriting historical sales, calls, other visuals or backend rules.
- Company rankings render one seller column per company with a shared 32rem maximum width for its header and seller list on all chart surfaces; `tvLayout` prioritizes seller rows over chart/Calls whitespace only in fitted TV boards. `tvPresentationDefault` gives seller boards MQL's 60% zoom with fitting retained and an independent v6 device preference; `tvStageSizing` expands the canvas without compensating away intentional reduction. Why: compact cards and complete rows must fit without changing other boards, saved layouts, data or explicit URL overrides.
- Fitted TV rows measure natural Calls seller-list height plus card chrome in layout pixels through `useTvRowContentHeight`, feeding row minimums and the shared stage's required height; observers handle data, wrapping and viewport changes. The stage's `fitted` context keeps Calls geometry stable during measurement, while stacked bars retain legend/plot space. Why: stacked sellers must fit completely without density oscillation, internal scrolling, stale two-column floors or changes to saved layouts and non-TV surfaces.
- Vertical stacked charts keep the legend in normal flex flow above a bounded plot and cap column widths; labels never intercept bar clicks. Why: actual legend height prevents overlaps at any zoom without changing saved layouts or aggregations.
- All ranking layouts resolve accents through `insightPaletteColors`, including compact company columns; product pills keep their separate presentation. Why: per-visual palette changes must reach progress bars and row accents without changing product colors or persisted data.
- Calls Commercial share one vertically stacked, left-aligned bounded seller section per row across fitted TV, manual zoom, normal, preview and shared charts; `rankCallCommercialSellers` orders by visible sales, then completed calls, then name without mutating aggregates (year uses completed calls). Identity stays above bottom-aligned metric blocks (number, label, conversion); density uses untransformed client dimensions and overflow stays local. Why: seller hierarchy must be consistent without stretching blocks or changing dashboard order, counts, scopes or saved settings.
