Why: clients remain shared and historical sales must not be reclassified from the current product catalog.

History lives in `bulk_deal_operations`/`_items`; own latest move undo uses locked snapshots, a complete server count and an atomic invoker update, never client-supplied before values. Why: audit and undo must not widen access, replay closing effects, overwrite later changes or partially restore a batch.

- Pipeline workspace keeps search and pipeline visible; secondary filters live in a demand-open Sheet, and nested filter/calendar popovers portal inside it. Why: filter density must not consume board height or break modal focus and scrolling.
- Kanban edge navigation observes only its horizontal scroller and content, shows a right arrow while more stages remain and flips to a left arrow at the end, hides when there is no overflow or during dragging, and respects reduced motion. Why: overflow cues must not intercept card scrolling or deal dragging.
- Pipeline ordering is a cancellable draft in the selector, saved through existing authenticated `pipelines.display_order` updates scoped to account, active row and previous order; only visible slots change and failures reconcile from the server. Why: organizing funnels must preserve access, hidden funnels, selected pipeline and all deal/stage data without new backend rules.

- The subscription question at win time is driven by `companies.requires_subscription_on_win` (never the company name) against the deal's stored `company_id`; the answer lives in `deals.is_subscription`/`subscription_cycle`, the allowed "Forma da Pagamento" values per cycle live only in `src/lib/sales/subscriptionWin.ts`, and every win entry point checks `fetchSubscriptionWinState` (Pipeline and RoyZapp in their own win flow, other screens through `DealDetailSheet`'s gate) and collects it in `RequiredFieldsModal`. Why: one rule for all win paths that survives company renames and new companies.
- Deal activity indicators (`get_deal_activity_stats`, RoyZapp deal task lists) count a task on its own deal only; tasks with no deal still count through the shared lead/client. Why: a pending task of another deal of the same contact painted cards overdue with nothing to do.
- In `RequiredFieldsModal`, when the subscription question applies, every other win field stays hidden until it is answered (and the cycle chosen when it is a subscription); saving is still validated by `isSubscriptionAnswerComplete`. Why: payment method options depend on the answer, so filling them first breaks the flow.
- The deal card's "Assinaturas" (RykaPay) tab is gated by `canShowPaySubscriptions` (src/lib/sales/paySubscriptionsTab.ts) and its content mounts only when shown. Why: before the win there is no subscription, so nothing should be displayed or queried.
- In the win flow, a subscription never shows the payment breakdown form (`sellerFillsBreakdown` in `RequiredFieldsModal`): once the cycle is chosen it reads RykaPay through the existing `rykapay-read` path and `paidPayCharge` (src/lib/sales/subscriptionPayCharge.ts) supplies value and first-payment date read-only, stored as one charge (`withoutInstallmentChoice`), only for a complete answer with a paid charge of that cycle; otherwise nothing is stored and a notice points to the Assinaturas tab. Why: billing lives in RykaPay, and unknown or partial data must never be typed in or shown as a confirmed payment.
- The paid-win lock applies only when the deal's Item da Venda resolves to the Clínica Ryka program (`requiresPaidWin` in `src/lib/sales/payWinGate.ts`): `fetchSubscriptionWinState` reports such deals as incomplete so every win path opens `RequiredFieldsModal`, where the RykaPay read runs only on the seller's click (saving the typed document first, since the read uses stored deal data) and `payWinGate` releases the remaining fields, the briefing tab and saving only on a confirmed payment. Why: one choke point for all win entries, no override, and other products never touch RykaPay.
- The Assinaturas tab keeps its RykaPay read while the card is open and stops automatic re-reads once `payReadSettled` (src/lib/sales/paySettled.ts) says nothing can change; the refresh button still reads, and the win flow uses a separate, never-kept read. Why: a paid-off plan must not keep calling RykaPay, while the win lock always needs a fresh answer.
- Deal activity bulk selection: "Marcar todas" resolves its ids only through `selectAllActivityIds` (src/lib/sales/activitySelection.ts), which returns pending activities; completed ones are selected one by one. Why: a bulk delete of pending touches once erased the deal's completed history, which has no undo.
- A payment made by someone else never bypasses the paid-win lock: the seller asks through `request_pay_link`, a manager (`can_manage_spiff_payments`) picks one sale from the full ownerless list served by `rykapay-orphans` and states the payer's relationship, and `decide_pay_link` stores the sale↔deal link plus the third-party payer; `rykapay-read` then re-reads the provider and reports `paidBy`, and `payWinGate` releases only on a still-paid sale (a saved payer from another deal needs the seller's confirmation). Every confirmed win records its sale ids via `record_pay_sale_links`, which is what makes a sale "owned". Why: the manager points at a real payment instead of overriding the lock, and ownerless sales stay a short list.
- Stripe is legacy only: `pay_orphan_seed` is a closed list and `rykapay-read` accepts the provider's `stripe` block only for e-mails on it. Why: new sales are billed in RykaPay, so nothing new may enter through Stripe.

- The create-deal dialogs (Leads page and Pipeline's Leads tab) share `CreateDealExtras`: the optional seller becomes `responsible_user_id` (default: the creator) and the four optional sale fields are resolved from the account's deal custom fields by name through `pickCreateDealFields` (src/lib/sales/createDealExtras.ts) and written to `deal_field_values` after the insert, blank answers writing nothing; revenue suggests MQL through `mqlFromRevenue`. Why: one optional block for both dialogs, no hard-coded field ids and no required field added to creation.
