- Google Meet transcripts enter Videochamadas via `gdrive-sync-meet-transcripts`: reads each seller's own Drive connection ("Meet Recordings" folder), inserts `video_call_sessions` with `source='meet_drive'`, deduplicated by unique `(account_id, drive_file_id)`, status pending for the existing Analisar flow. Triggered by the seller's screen (`MeetDriveSyncBar`, ≤1 per 30 min) or `x-cron-secret`. Why: per-user Google consent, no shared credentials, never imports the same file twice.
- Pending `meet_drive` sessions are analyzed without a click by `meet-call-auto-analyze` (managed cron schedule), which claims rows atomically (`pending`→`analyzing`), writes `analysis`/`analysis_status`, and pauses the account in `meet_analysis_state` on AI credit/access failures. Prompt, models and output limits live once in `supabase/functions/_shared/salesCallAnalysis.ts`, shared with the manual `analyze-sales-call`. Why: one prompt for both routes and no double analysis.
- Pipeline company filtering is local, user-persisted, independent of the global company selection and shared across open/won/lost through `filterDealsByCompany`; it reads only each deal's stored `company_id` from the RLS-scoped query. Why: clients remain shared and historical sales must not be reclassified from the current product catalog.
- Pipeline bulk selection is restricted to the filtered, RLS-scoped deals and snapshots explicit confirmation; `useBulkDeals` runs one checked write per record, reuses `moveDeal` and required-field validation, reports failures/cancellation without retries, and refreshes field maps after completion. Why: bulk editing must not widen access, bypass sales flows, or silently include hidden records.
- Individual and bulk revenue-field edits use `mqlFromRevenue` in `src/lib/sales/bulkDeals.ts`. Why: qualification rules must stay identical across both editing paths.
