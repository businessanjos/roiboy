- A payment made by someone else never bypasses the paid-win lock: the seller asks through `request_pay_link`, a manager (`can_manage_spiff_payments`) picks one sale from the full ownerless list served by `rykapay-orphans` and states the payer's relationship, and `decide_pay_link` stores the sale↔deal link plus the third-party payer; `rykapay-read` then re-reads the provider and reports `paidBy`, and `payWinGate` releases only on a still-paid sale (a saved payer from another deal needs the seller's confirmation). Why: the manager points at a real payment instead of overriding the lock, and ownerless sales stay a short list.
