# AGENTS.md — src/hooks
- Accelerometer counters and records share `useAccelerometerSales` (complete RLS-scoped won-deal reads, saved Item da Venda identity, stable monthly IDs); program goals read existing product-specific quantities only. Why: the dial and drilldown agree without mixing programs into mentorship, inventing goals or changing payouts.
- Program meeting counts and drilldowns share `useAccelerometerMeetings` task IDs, matched through a visible non-deleted linked deal's saved Item da Venda; absent goals keep the shared dial without scale or percentages. Why: shared contacts must not misattribute mentorship calls or imply an invented target.
- CS process access is shared by `useCsProcessAccess`: editing comes from `is_cs_process_editor`, all-client visibility also accepts the existing `cs/processos_ver_todas` grant. Why: the controls must match content RLS without promoting anyone or changing global management rules.
- Content archive reads every source page through the current user's client and RLS. Why: one read-only catalogue without copies, scraper jobs or changed access.

- Notification INSERTs delivered late (tab asleep, browser back, realtime reconnect) only refresh the bell once; sound/toast/push fire only for fresh events, at most one per 1.2s. Why: a backlog flush used to interrupt for every event and froze the screen.
- RoyZapp tag badges update live: `zapp_conversation_tags` and `zapp_tags` are in the `supabase_realtime` publication and `useZappConversations` patches `assignments.conversation_tags` from those events. Why: the badge next to the name must change without reloading the screen.

- RoyZapp media uploads retry transient storage failures through `withZappRetry` (src/lib/zappTransientErrors.ts) and treat "already exists" as success; WhatsApp send calls are not auto-retried, and errors shown to the team go through `friendlyZappError`. Why: a storage 504 must not surface raw "Unexpected token <" text, and retrying the send itself could deliver twice.
