- Builds the Vendas "Assinaturas" dashboard read: won deals of the Clínica Ryka program (read under the caller's RLS, managers or people granted the `comercial/subscriptions` permission item, via `can_view_pay_portfolio`) are looked up in RykaPay by their own documents, then e-mails, approved third-party payers and linked sales; returns only clinic name, seller, dates and amounts. Why: one portfolio-wide read with the shared key that never widens deal visibility or exposes documents, e-mails or tokens.
- A contract counts only with at least one paid invoice, belongs to one clinic, and provider failures set `partial` instead of becoming zeros; nothing is written. Why: abandoned checkouts are not sales and an incomplete read must never look like lost revenue.
- A won deal without a located payment carries `releasedBy` when the win flow's release note (`PAY_RELEASE_NOTE_TITLE`) was written by someone who passes `can_manage_spiff_payments`; `unmatchedClinics` drops those from "Pagamentos sem dono", and they still stay out of the numbers. Why: the manager who released the win took the case on, but no revenue is invented.
